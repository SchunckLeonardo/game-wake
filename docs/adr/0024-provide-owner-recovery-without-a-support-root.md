# Provide Owner Recovery without a support root

GameWake keeps Discord as the ordinary MVP sign-in but requires a sole Owner to verify a recovery email and store one-time recovery codes before the account's first payment. Accounts with multiple Owners may recover by having another Owner replace the lost Linked Identity. Support never transfers ownership based only on a message or Discord username, and every recovery notifies all Owners, providing a break-glass path without creating an implicit support root.
